Uncertainty Quantification in ML-Based Building Energy Modeling — Systematic Review

Reviews 141 studies on UQ in ML-based building energy modeling. Identifies three primary uncertainty sources, surveys ten state-of-the-art UQ techniques (BNNs, MCMC, variational inference, GP, etc.), and proposes a taxonomy of aleatoric vs. epistemic uncertainty. Published in Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews, 218, 2025.
