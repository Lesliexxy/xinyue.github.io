Education Award May 2019

Outstanding Graduate, Tongji University

Graduated from Tongji University's Built Environment and Energy Engineering department as an Outstanding Graduate — the university's top honor for graduating students.

Date May 2019
Degree B.Eng., Built Environment and Energy Engineering
Institution Tongji University, Shanghai, China
Honor Outstanding Graduate
[ assets/images/news/tongji-2019.jpg ]
Tongji University graduation, Shanghai — May 2019

In May 2019, Xinyue Xu graduated from the School of Mechanical Engineering at Tongji University (Shanghai, China), earning her Bachelor of Engineering in Built Environment and Energy Engineering. She was recognized as an Outstanding Graduate, an honor conferred to students who demonstrate exceptional academic performance, research contributions, and professional promise over the course of their undergraduate studies.

Tongji University is one of China's leading research universities, particularly renowned for its engineering and architecture programs. Xinyue's undergraduate training provided a strong foundation in building physics, thermodynamics, HVAC systems, and computational fluid dynamics — groundwork that informed her subsequent doctoral research at Penn State on building energy modeling and uncertainty quantification.

Upon graduation, Xinyue enrolled in the Ph.D. program in Architectural Engineering at The Pennsylvania State University in August 2019, supported by the University Graduate Fellowship and Singley Memorial Graduate Fellowship.
