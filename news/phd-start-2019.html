The Pennsylvania State University — Ph.D. started August 2019

In August 2019, Xinyue Xu began her doctoral studies in the Department of Architectural Engineering at The Pennsylvania State University. She joined the Wang Research Group under the advisement of Prof. Julian Wang, whose research focuses on uncertainty quantification, building energy modeling, and physics-informed machine learning for built environment systems.

Xinyue was supported at the outset by two competitive fellowships: the University Graduate Fellowship and the Singley Memorial Graduate Fellowship — recognitions of her strong academic background and research potential following her undergraduate education at Tongji University in Shanghai.

Her doctoral research has since developed into a distinctive body of work at the intersection of Bayesian inference, physics-guided neural networks, uncertainty quantification, and building energy modeling — spanning journal publications, conference presentations, and interdisciplinary collaborations.
