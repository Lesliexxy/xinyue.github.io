Presentation at the 12th IAQVEC, Los Angeles, CA — May 2026

Xinyue Xu presented research at the 12th International Conference on Indoor Air Quality, Ventilation and Energy Conservation in Buildings (IAQVEC) held in Los Angeles, California in May 2026. IAQVEC is a leading international forum for researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of indoor environment quality, ventilation systems, and building energy performance.

The presentation contributed to ongoing work in the Wang Research Group at Penn State on building energy modeling, uncertainty quantification, and physics-guided machine learning approaches for built environment applications.
