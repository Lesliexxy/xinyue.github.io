"Assessing building thermal resilience in response to heatwaves through integrating a social vulnerability lens" published in Journal of Building Engineering.

Read Paper ↗
Date 2024
Category Journal Paper
Journal Journal of Building Engineering
Authors S. Paneru, Xinyue Xu, Julian Wang, G. Chi, Y. Hu
DOI 10.1016/j.jobe.2024.111219
[ assets/images/news/jobe-2024.jpg ]
Key figure from Paneru et al. (2024), Journal of Building Engineering

This paper, led by S. Paneru with co-authorship by Xinyue Xu, examines how buildings respond thermally to extreme heatwave events while also accounting for the social vulnerability of their occupants. The work integrates building energy simulation with social vulnerability indices to assess disparities in thermal resilience across different building stocks and population groups.

The study contributes to a growing body of research on climate resilience and environmental justice in the built environment, highlighting that thermal comfort and safety during heat extremes are unevenly distributed and that socially vulnerable populations face compounded risk from both inadequate building performance and limited adaptive capacity.

Xinyue Xu contributed modeling and analysis expertise to this interdisciplinary collaboration within the Wang Research Group at Penn State.
