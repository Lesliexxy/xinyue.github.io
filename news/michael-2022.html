Michael Endowed Graduate Fellowship in Engineering

Awarded the Louis S. and Sara S. Michael Endowed Graduate Fellowship in Engineering ($3,000) by The Pennsylvania State University.

Date June 2022
Category Award / Fellowship
Amount $3,000
Institution The Pennsylvania State University
[ assets/images/news/michael-2022.jpg ]
Michael Endowed Graduate Fellowship, Penn State — June 2022

The Louis S. and Sara S. Michael Endowed Graduate Fellowship in Engineering is a competitive fellowship at The Pennsylvania State University recognizing graduate students in engineering who demonstrate exceptional academic achievement and research promise. Xinyue Xu received this $3,000 fellowship in June 2022, during her third year of Ph.D. studies in the Department of Architectural Engineering.

This fellowship honors the legacy of Louis S. and Sara S. Michael by supporting the next generation of engineering scholars. At the time of this award, Xinyue was advancing research on uncertainty quantification, Bayesian model calibration, and physics-guided machine learning, under the advisement of Prof. Julian Wang.
